About Scent Log

Brand Story

Scent Log Was Never
Perfect From the Start

Two experiments. Two lessons. And one truth discovered:
what people wanted wasn't a perfume — it was a guide to the right moment.

Phase 01
Odorable
Odor + Adorable — a product-first fragrance brand

We believed a great scent would sell itself. We launched with mass-produced products — and watched inventory pile up while capital froze. The structure couldn't account for each person's time, place, or occasion.

"What people wanted wasn't a perfume — it was a guide to the right moment."
Phase 02
Studio L'art
L'art (art) — private perfumery atelier, currently operating

After the failure, we shifted to a 1:1 approach. Listening to each person's style, lifestyle, and daily rhythm before crafting their scent revealed our core insight. Still operating today, Studio L'art is the origin of Scent Log's entire methodology.

"You have to understand a person's day first."
Phase 03 — Now
Scent Log
Scent + Log — a time-based custom scent design platform

Structurally solving Odorable's inventory risk while scaling Studio L'art's 1:1 expertise nationwide. Not product sales — a shift to time-based custom scent design. That is Scent Log.

"Without the failures, there would be no Scent Log."
